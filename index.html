Photo Sorter

Find the people you choose in your photos and copy their pictures into their own folders. Everything stays on your computer.

Loading face recognition, this takes a few seconds the first time...

1 Choose the folders to scan

Add every folder you want searched (for example all 4 wedding folders on your external drive). They are scanned one after the other. Subfolders are included. Your original photos are never changed or moved.

2 Add the people you want

For each person, add 3 to 5 photos where their face is clear, ideally from different angles. If a photo has more than one face, you'll be asked to click the right one.

3 Set up your folders

Each person gets their own folder automatically. You can rename or remove those, and add as many extra folders as you like, each with the people you choose. You can change this at any time, even after scanning.

4 Scan all photos

This looks for faces in every photo in every folder from step 1, one folder after the other. For a few thousand photos it can take 15 to 30 minutes. Keep this tab open. You can add or change people and folders afterwards without scanning again.

5 Review the matches

Green photos will be copied. Click a photo to remove it (or add it back). Use "View" to see it bigger.

Looser Stricter

Looser finds more photos but makes more mistakes. Stricter makes fewer mistakes but may miss some photos.

6 Save the folders

Choose where to save. A folder called "Sorted by person" will be created there, with all your folders from step 3 inside it. Photos are copied, not moved.
